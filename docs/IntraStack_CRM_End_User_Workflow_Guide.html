01 | Purpose, Scope and Operating Model

The IntraStack CRM Platform is the operational system of record from the first customer interaction through commercial approval, staffing or consulting delivery, project execution and renewal.

What the platform controls

Customer truth

Companies, decision makers, operational contacts, candidates, vendors, communication details and relationship tags.

Commercial truth

Opportunity owner, pipeline, stage, expected value, source, urgency, quotation, contract dates and confirmed Sales Order.

Staffing truth

Requirements, recruiters, candidate submissions, interviews, placement approvals, rates and gross margin.

Delivery truth

Delivery project, template tasks, owners, deadlines, timesheets, commercial traceability and renewal dates.

One record, one purposeA CRM opportunity represents the commercial deal. A staffing requirement represents a specific role to fill. A candidate submission represents one candidate against one requirement. A project represents delivery after the commercial commitment is confirmed.

04 | Customer and Contact Database

The Contacts application is the shared customer database. A company is stored once and its people are created as related contacts. Candidates and recruiter vendors are also contacts, distinguished by relationship and tags.

Create a customer company

1Open Contacts and select New

Select Company. Search first to confirm that the organization does not already exist.

2Enter reliable identity data

Complete company name, website, phone, address, country, Tax ID where applicable and the responsible salesperson.

3Add people under the company

Use the Contacts & Addresses area to create decision makers, procurement, technical and operational contacts. Add job title, direct email and mobile number.

4Apply relationship tags

Tags enable filtering and management reporting. Use only approved tags and avoid creating spelling variants.

Current production Contacts list: use search, filters and grouping to locate customers and relationship types.

Contact quality checklist

  • Company record exists only once.
  • Individual contact is linked to the correct company.
  • Email and telephone formats are valid.
  • Job title and relationship role are current.
  • Decision maker status on the opportunity reflects the primary deal contact.
  • Internal notes do not contain unnecessary sensitive personal information.
Current contact form. Company and individual records together form the customer history.

How to find a customer's contact information

  1. Open Contacts from the application selector.
  2. Use the search field for company name, person name, email or phone.
  3. Use filters such as Companies, Individuals or a relationship tag.
  4. Open the company record and review the Contacts & Addresses section for stakeholders.
  5. From an opportunity, click the Customer field or contact link to return to the shared customer database.

05 | CSV/XLSX Import and Export

Odoo 17 Community supports CSV and XLSX import. The controlled templates shipped with the platform use External IDs so the same file can update existing records without creating duplicates.

Download a template from the Import screen

After opening Import records, use the Need Help? panel to download the relevant IntraStack Template. Replace the example rows, preserve the header row, and upload the completed file on the same screen. Contacts provides separate templates for companies, people, candidates and recruiter vendors; CRM, Employees and each Staffing Operations record type provide their own template. Odoo's standard templates remain available, but the IntraStack templates are recommended because they include the platform's custom BRD fields and External ID relationships.

Current production Import screen. The Need Help? panel exposes the standard Odoo template and four IntraStack templates.

Exact import procedure

  1. Open the target application and switch to List view. Import is not normally available from Kanban or Form view.
  2. Open the list action menu (the small dropdown beside the list controls) and select Import records.
  3. In Need Help?, click the template for the record type. For a customer load, download Customer Companies first, then Company Contacts.
  4. Open the downloaded CSV/XLSX and replace only the example values. Keep the header names, External IDs, date format and selection values controlled.
  5. Upload the file, inspect every automatic field mapping, and manually correct any field with a warning or no match.
  6. Select Test. Do not select Import until the test reports no errors and the expected row count is correct.
  7. After importing, search by External ID, open a sample record and verify relational links before loading the next dependent file.
Production safetyUse a staging database for the first rehearsal. The production screen contains real customer and employee information; never use a real customer export as a training file.

Required import order

  1. Customer companies.
  2. Individual contacts linked through Related Company/External ID.
  3. CRM opportunities linked through Customer/External ID.
  4. Staffing requirements linked through CRM Opportunity and Customer External IDs.

Import steps

1Prepare and validate the file

Use one header row, ISO dates YYYY-MM-DD, numeric amounts without currency symbols and approved selection labels.

2Open the target list and select Import records

Upload the file and inspect the preview. If the UI language differs from English, manually map each field.

3Map relational fields by External ID

Do not match customers by approximate names when a stable External ID is available.

4Select Test

Resolve every error before Import. Never ignore a row-count mismatch.

5Import and reconcile

Record the file name, operator, import date and expected row count. Search/export the imported batch and compare totals.

08 | Opportunity Operating Procedure

Create and qualify

1Search Contacts first

Create or update the customer and primary contact before creating the deal.

2Create the opportunity

Use the appropriate pipeline menu or the general Pipeline. Enter customer, salesperson and opportunity name.

3Classify the deal

Complete classification, service category, urgency, source, expected value and decision maker status. The team/stage will route automatically.

4Record the next action

Schedule an activity with an owner and deadline. Add discovery notes and files in the Chatter.

5Progress using evidence

Move stages only when the required customer action, deliverable or approval exists.

Quick create versus full form

Current quick-create card. Use it only for initial capture, then select Edit/open the opportunity to complete all IntraStack fields.
  1. Select New in Kanban to open the quick-create card.
  2. Enter Organization/Contact, Opportunity, Email, Phone and initial Expected Revenue.
  3. Select Edit when the record requires classification, service, urgency, source, decision-maker, contract or delivery fields.
  4. After saving, verify the opportunity moved to the team and first stage matching Deal Classification.

Opportunity header and smart buttons

ControlPurpose
New Quotation / QuotationsCreate or review linked quotations and Sales Orders.
Won / LostClose the commercial outcome. Won is validated against mandatory commercial data.
StaffingOpen Staffing Requirements related to a Staffing opportunity.
Delivery ProjectOpen the project generated from a confirmed order. It appears only when a project exists.
ActivitiesSchedule the next call, email, meeting or to-do with a deadline.
Send message / Log noteCommunicate with followers or record internal-only operational context.

Commercial readiness before confirmation

The Sales Order cannot be confirmed until all of the following are complete:

  • Customer.
  • Deal Classification.
  • Service Category.
  • Urgency Flag.
  • Source Tracking.
  • Expected Value greater than zero.
  • Contract Start and Contract End.
  • At least one service product order line.
  • Quotation customer matches the opportunity customer.

Moving an opportunity directly to a Won stage also invokes readiness validation. This prevents incomplete contracts from entering delivery.

Lost opportunities

When a deal is no longer viable, mark it Lost and choose a meaningful reason. Add a concise internal note describing the decision, competitor or timing issue. Do not leave dead opportunities indefinitely in an active stage.

Activity hygiene

  • Complete an activity only after performing the action.
  • Schedule the next activity before leaving an active opportunity.

11 | Delivery Projects and Timesheets

Confirming a qualifying Sales Order creates one delivery project. If Odoo's standard service-product project already exists, the integration reuses it instead of creating a duplicate.

Project templates

Service lineTemplateOperating intent
StaffingStaffing DeliveryOnboarding, assignment readiness, ongoing coordination and closeout.
ConsultingConsulting DeliveryDiscovery, design, implementation, acceptance and handover tasks.
Managed ServicesManaged Service EngagementOnboarding, service transition, SLA operation and review tasks.
SubcontractingStandard projectProject created without a dedicated BRD template; PM defines tasks for the engagement.
Current project template task board. Owners, deadlines and stages should show the true delivery state.

Project Manager procedure

  1. Open the project from the CRM opportunity or confirmed Sales Order.
  2. Verify customer, opportunity and order links.
  3. Review template tasks, remove only tasks that are truly out of scope and add project-specific deliverables.
  4. Assign every active task to an accountable user with a deadline.
  5. Record risks, decisions and acceptance evidence in task/project Chatter.
  6. Review timesheets, overdue tasks and milestones at least weekly.

Timesheet procedure

  • Record time against the correct project and task, preferably daily.
  • Use a clear description of the work performed, not a generic label such as “work”.
  • Do not record time before the authorized contract/project start.
  • Project Managers approve or correct time before billing/reporting cutoffs.
Current Projects screen. Open the linked delivery project to manage tasks and record timesheets.

13 | Staffing Requirements

Requirement states

DraftOpenSourcingInterviewOfferFilledOn HoldCancelled

Create a requirement

  1. Open a Staffing-classified opportunity and use the Staffing smart button, or open CRM → Staffing Operations → Requirements.
  2. Select the CRM Opportunity. The customer and contract dates may default from the opportunity.
  3. Enter Requirement name, Role / Job Title, required skills, positions and recruiter.
  4. Enter location, work arrangement (On-site/Hybrid/Remote) and engagement type (Contract/Contract to Hire/Permanent).
  5. Enter expected start/end and target fill date.
  6. Enter target bill rate, target cost rate and rate unit. Review calculated Target Margin and Target Margin %.
  7. Add acceptance criteria and job description; attach supporting requirement documents where appropriate.
  8. Select Open, then Start Sourcing when recruitment begins.
Current Requirement form with status buttons, smart buttons, customer linkage, role and commercial target sections.

Requirement field instructions

Field/groupOperator instruction
CRM OpportunitySelect the Staffing opportunity that originated this role. The server rejects non-Staffing opportunities.
Customer / ContactSelect the customer company and operational stakeholder. The contact must belong to that customer.
Recruiter / PositionsAssign an active internal recruiter and enter the actual number of openings.
Role & EngagementComplete job title, skills, work arrangement, engagement type, location and dates.
Commercial TargetsEnter bill and cost rates, select the rate unit and review calculated margin before sourcing.
Status controlsUse the workflow buttons; do not edit status through imports merely to skip approvals.

Requirement controls

  • Positions must be greater than zero.
  • Target cost must not exceed target bill rate.
  • Expected End and target dates must be logically consistent with Expected Start.
  • The linked opportunity must be an opportunity classified as Staffing.
  • Filled/open positions are computed from active confirmed placements.
Requirement versus opportunityCreate multiple requirements under one customer opportunity when the customer requests different roles, locations or rate structures. Do not overload one requirement with unrelated jobs.

14 | Candidate Submissions

Submission states

SourcingScreeningSubmittedInterviewOfferAcceptedRejectedWithdrawn

Create and progress a submission

  1. Create or locate the candidate as an individual Contact. Search by email before creating a new candidate.
  2. From the Requirement, add a Candidate Submission.
  3. Select source: Internal Talent Pool, Referral, Recruiter/Vendor, LinkedIn, Job Board or Other.
  4. If sourced through a vendor, select the Recruiter Vendor.
  5. Upload Resume/CV and enter availability, screening summary and internal notes.
  6. Enter proposed bill and cost rates. Verify positive margin.
  7. Select Start Screening and document screening results.
  8. Select Submit when the profile has been sent to the customer; the submitted date is recorded.
  9. Move to Interview or Offer based on customer progression. Use Reject/Withdraw with a clear note when appropriate.

Submission quality standard

  • Candidate identity and contact details are verified.
  • Current resume is attached.
  • Availability and location/work arrangement fit the requirement.
  • Screening summary addresses mandatory skills.
  • Bill/cost rates are commercially viable.
  • Duplicate active submission for the same candidate/requirement is avoided.
Current Candidate Submission form with source, recruiter/vendor, resume, screening, proposed rates, margin and workflow controls.

16 | Placements and Margin Control

Placement states

DraftPending ApprovalConfirmedActiveCompletedCancelled

Create a placement

  1. Use an accepted/offered Candidate Submission from the correct Requirement.
  2. Enter Placement Start/End, bill rate, cost rate, rate unit and hours per week.
  3. Link the confirmed Sales Order or enter a signed Contract/SOW reference.
  4. Verify the customer on the order/project matches the requirement customer.
  5. Review Gross Margin, Gross Margin %, Weekly Revenue and Weekly Margin.
  6. Select Submit for Approval, then Confirm after commercial approval.
  7. Select Activate only on or after the contract start date.
  8. Select Complete at the end of service; the system records an end date if one is missing.

Placement validation rules

  • Bill and cost rates cannot be negative.
  • Bill rate must exceed or equal cost rate.
  • A confirmed Sales Order is required when an order is linked.
  • A contract/SOW reference or Sales Order is required before confirmation.
  • Candidate submission and placement must belong to the same requirement.
  • Placement cannot become Active before Placement Start.
  • Customer must match across requirement, quotation and project.
Current Placement form. Use Draft → Submit for Approval → Confirm Placement → Start Placement.
Automatic operational synchronizationConfirming a placement marks the candidate submission Accepted and updates the requirement's filled/open position counts.

Detailed operating steps

  1. Create/update the customer and stakeholder contacts.
  2. Create an opportunity with Deal Classification Consulting and Service Category Cloud.
  3. During Discovery, document business objectives, current-state environment, stakeholders, constraints and target outcomes.
  4. During Qualification, validate budget, authority, need, timeline, data/security constraints and procurement path.

18 | Other Service Workflows

Subcontracting

  1. Create a company Contact for the partner/vendor and apply approved vendor-related tags.
  2. Create a Subcontracting opportunity and assign the relationship owner.
  3. Progress Outreach → Vendor Qualification → Capability Review.
  4. Document certifications, capability, commercial constraints, references and risk.
  5. Move to Approved Vendor only after required business approval.
  6. For a live engagement, create/confirm the commercial document and use the generated standard project for delivery coordination.

Managed Services

  1. Qualify customer fit and perform the infrastructure/service assessment.
  2. Define service scope, coverage hours, response targets, exclusions, onboarding and reporting cadence.
  3. Create the Managed Services quotation containing onboarding, monthly service and additional-hours lines.
  4. Enter contract and renewal review dates before confirmation.

22 | Data Governance and Security

Minimum governance rules

  • Search before creating customers, contacts, candidates, opportunities and requirements.
  • Use approved tags, selections and naming standards.
  • Do not delete records that are referenced by quotations, projects, staffing records or timesheets.
  • Use Archive for obsolete master data and Lost/Cancelled for terminated workflows.
  • Do not export customer/candidate data without a legitimate business purpose.
  • Keep passwords, API keys, infrastructure secrets and private credentials outside Odoo records and attachments.
  • Do not grant access manually when the Employee role workflow can be used.

Chatter guidance

Use Chatter forDo not use Chatter for
Customer decisions, meeting outcomes, approved assumptions, delivery evidence, internal handoff notes.Passwords, secrets, unverified allegations, excessive personal data or off-topic conversation.

Audit readiness

A complete business record should show who created/changed it, the customer context, current owner, next action, stage history, commercial document, contract dates and downstream delivery record.
